# Writing Guide — how the points and content are written

This guide explains how every piece of text in the competitor review is written. It's based on the reviewer's own corrections in the original session. **Before → After** examples show real changes the reviewer asked for. Read it together with `HANDOFF.md`, part 2.

---

## 1. The overall voice

- **Observe; don't judge.** Describe what a page does and how it is set up, and what that could mean for a player. Don't declare winners or losers.
- **Soft, measured language.** The reviewer writes things like "can feel overwhelming", "could be confusing", "makes it unclear whether…", "might not immediately realise…". Follow that tone. Avoid absolute statements like "poor", "bad", "fails", "best" or "must".
- **About structure and setup.** Talk about layout, navigation, flows, where features live and how they're presented. Never about offer amounts, prize sizes, how many items there are, or which promotion types a casino runs. Screenshots never capture everything, so counts aren't reliable.
- **Short.** Card bullets are phrases, not sentences. Comparison text is one or two sentences.
- **Plain words.** Use the player's point of view ("players", "the player"). Quote the site's own labels in single quotes: 'Rewards', 'Bonus Store', 'Take it'.
- **Casino names as branded:** VegasNova, Golden Panda, HertzBetz, Housebets, Oliver Casino (or Oliver), Sir Betalot, SlotMonster, Slotorado.

## 2. The three cards

### Works (green)
Short positive phrases about what the subject casino does well, written as facts.

**Pattern:** a noun phrase, sometimes followed by its benefit.
- "Clear overall layout"
- "Strong banner that uses the space well"
- "Points balance visible"
- "Own page keeps missions clearly separate from the store"
- "Tapping Deposit opens the payment methods straight away"

**Rules**
- If the reviewer sees something as a valid choice, it belongs here, not under Potential gaps. For example:
  - "Simple filter that matches the current promotion types"
  - "Points feed straight into the Rewards Store"
- If the section doesn't exist at all, leave the card empty and the page shows "Not offered".

### Potential gaps (red)
Short, factual phrases about structure that could cause friction. The card title is always **"Potential gaps"**, never "Gaps".

**Pattern:** state what is there, or missing, without blame.
- "'Rewards' appears twice in the side menu"
- "Missions & Store hidden in a Rewards dropdown"
- "'Bonus Store' duplicates Store (old widget)"
- "No bonus shown or offered, even for new players"
- "Hard to find inside the Rewards dropdown"

**Rules**
- **One root cause, one line.** Don't add lines that only follow from it.
  - Before: "No tournaments or races" · "No leaderboards" · "Nothing in the menu"
  - After: "No tournaments or races" · "No leaderboards"
- **Soften points that depend on the future.**
  - Before: "Only one filter ('Casino Bonuses')"
  - After: "Single filter won't scale if more promotion types are added"
- **Not gaps.** The reviewer removed every one of these:
  - mission rewards that differ in size (they're proportional to wagering)
  - "Value of points not shown" (the store shows what points buy)
  - "Same artwork on every item"
  - "Off-brand background image"
  - "No 'next reward' nudge"
  - "No Rewards entry in the bottom bar"
  - missions and store being on separate pages (a valid design choice)
- **Not yet confirmed?** Don't list it. Raise it with the reviewer, or show it as "to check" in a comparison. Only list it once confirmed. Example: the cashier bonus was "to check" until the reviewer tested with a new account.

### Ideas to consider (purple, numbered)
Suggestions, not instructions. The card title is always **"Ideas to consider"**, never "Do next" or "Recommendations".

**Pattern:** start with the subject of the change, then use "could", "Consider…", "… is worth testing" or "… is another option".
- "Consider removing 'Bonus Store'"
- "Missions and Store could become their own menu items"
- "'Rewards' could appear only once in the menu"
- "The welcome bonus could be shown in the cashier"
- "A clear 'bonus / no bonus' choice could be offered"
- "Achievements / badges could add another layer alongside the store"

**Rules**
- **Each idea should answer a listed potential gap, or build on something that works.**
- **Keep ideas general. Don't design the feature.**
  - Before: "Launch a free weekly slot race" · "Add a daily free-spins race"
  - After: "Tournaments could be added"
- **Never suggest what the casino already does.**
  - Removed: "Keep missions in their own, easy-to-find section", because VegasNova already does this.
- **Drop the idea when its gap is dropped.**
  - Removed together with their gaps: "Show what each reward is worth", "Starter and streak missions are worth considering", "Real game artwork could make items more appealing", and "A Rewards entry in the bottom bar could put missions and store one tap away".

**Before → After wording**
| Before | After |
|---|---|
| "Remove 'Bonus Store'" | "Consider removing 'Bonus Store'" |
| "Show 'Rewards' only once" | "'Rewards' could appear only once in the menu" |
| "Merge Store + Bonus Store" | "Store and Bonus Store could be merged into one" |
| "Add levels for every player" | "Consider levels that every player can progress through" |

## 3. Comparison points ("How it compares")

### Title
2–6 words naming the topic, not the verdict.
- Good: "Filters" · "Full promotion titles" · "Cashier layout" · "How the store is presented" · "What missions lead to" · "What happens when tapping Deposit"
- Avoid verdict titles such as "VegasNova's weak filters" or "Best leaderboard".

### Text (1–2 sentences)
**Pattern:** what the subject casino does, then what some competitors do.
- "VegasNova has a single filter, 'Casino Bonuses'. That may simply be because it only runs one type of promotion right now. Sir Betalot shows how filters can grow once there are more types."
- "VegasNova opens the payment methods straight away. On SlotMonster, tapping Deposit first shows a pop-up asking the player to verify their ID before the cashier opens."

**Rules**
- **"Some competitors", never "the best competitors".**
  - Before: "The best competitors show a live leaderboard and where the player stands."
  - After: "Some competitors show a live leaderboard and where the player stands."
- **No rankings or superlatives, even mild ones.**
  - Before: "Sir Betalot is one of the clearest layouts…"
  - After: "Sir Betalot has a clean layout, with the banner visible straight away and every menu section listed."
- **Present different approaches as alternatives when both are valid.** Use "takes a different approach" or "Both approaches work".
  - Before: "VegasNova splits Missions, Store and Bonus Store into separate pages. Competitors bring everything together in one place."
  - After: "VegasNova keeps Missions and Store on their own pages, which keeps the purpose of each clear. They are just hard to find inside the Rewards dropdown. SlotMonster and Golden Panda take a different approach and show missions together with races, the store and more in one rewards hub."
- **Give the subject credit where it already does something.**
  - Before: "Golden Panda gives missions a purpose beyond points: badges to collect and a mini-game to play."
  - After: "Both give missions a long-term purpose. VegasNova's mission points are collected and spent in the Rewards Store. Golden Panda takes a different approach, with badges to collect and a mini-game to play."
- **Respect the reviewer's taste on visual points.**
  - Before: "VegasNova's banner fills most of the screen" (gap)
  - After: "VegasNova's banner is large but well used: offer, CTA and mascots fill the space." Contrast it with "Oliver's banner is just as big but has a lot of empty space."
- **Mention uncertainty plainly:** "worth checking", "so check what a new player sees".
- **A feature that sends players elsewhere** (promo cards for missions or the rewards hub) gets a balanced sentence: "It can help discovery, but it blurs the line between promotions and missions."

### Box labels (the numbered highlights)
3–8 words describing what is inside the box. Write facts, not opinions.
- "Missions as its own menu item" · "Code pre-filled" · "Bonus already selected, swipe for others" · "Prize per rank" · "Titles cut off mid-sentence" · "One filter, matching one promotion type"

### Tags on each screenshot
- **Subject:**
  - "works" for something good
  - "potential gap" for a confirmed issue
  - "to check" for something not confirmed
  - "mixed" for something partly good
- **Competitors:**
  - "Example" for a good example
  - "Mixed" for something with pros and cons
  - "Avoid" only for something clearly bad
  - "For reference" for a neutral fact with no judgement

## 4. Screenshot titles
Short and descriptive: what page or state the screenshot shows. Leave out amounts and counts.
- "Lobby with welcome-offer banner" · "Side menu open" · "Cashier: bonus selected, amount" · "Tapping Deposit: ID verification first" · "Missions tab in rewards hub"
- Before: "Weekly leaderboard, $50,000 USDT pool" → After: "Weekly leaderboard"
- Before: "Achievements tab: 0/17 collected" → After: "Achievements tab"

---

## 5. Applying the reviewer's notes

- **Apply notes literally and keep the reviewer's meaning.** If they say "I wouldn't call it the clearest, just clean", change the wording everywhere it appears: comparisons, casino descriptions, and anything else.
- **When the reviewer removes a point, also remove what depended on it:**
  - the matching idea
  - highlight boxes on screenshots that make the same point
  - lines elsewhere that repeat it
- **Carry corrections forward.** A rule given for one section or version applies to the others too. For example, "no counts" applies to all sections, and desktop corrections apply to mobile.
- **Check each claim against the version you're writing.** Points can differ between desktop and mobile. For example, VegasNova's promotion titles are cut off on desktop but shown in full on mobile.
- **If a note changes a section's overall picture, say so in your reply,** and ask before changing anything the reviewer didn't mention.
